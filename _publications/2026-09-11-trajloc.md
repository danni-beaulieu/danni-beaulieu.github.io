**Tianyi Gao, Jiayu Lin, Danni Beaulieu, Nathan Jacobs**

TrajLoc addresses spatially grounded retrieval across viewpoints and modalities: matching sequential first-person observations to overhead imagery. Alongside video, it introduces a new kind of query: first-person descriptions of a route.

To support this, we built **SeqGeo-VL**, a dataset pairing videos, route descriptions, trajectories, and satellite imagery, and trained a multimodal model on all four modalities. Grounding vision–language embeddings in trajectory geometry lets TrajLoc capture spatial structure beyond semantics and makes it more robust to weather-related visual degradation.

We also release **SeqGeo-VL-QA**, a benchmark adapted from SeqGeo-VL for evaluating how well multimodal LLMs understand space across views and modalities. The project page has interactive demos comparing TrajLoc with GPT-5.4 mini.

Presented as Poster #204 at ECCV 2026 in Malmö, Sweden.
